About

HackUpdate is a student-facing noticeboard maintained for the Cyber Security and Digital Forensics programmes at the National Forensic Sciences University. A scheduled crawl runs once a day and rebuilds the listing from public sources.

How to use it

  • Press / to jump to search.
  • Bookmark an event to keep it in the "Bookmarked" view on this device.
  • Use "Add to calendar" to get a reminder before registration closes.
  • Share an event by copying its link; it opens straight to that entry.

Add a source

Missing an event? Add the organiser's page or RSS feed to crawler/config/sources.json and it is picked up on the next run. Tune what counts as relevant in crawler/config/taxonomy.json.

Listings are gathered automatically from public websites and are provided for information only. Dates, fees, eligibility and rules can change without notice. Always verify on the organiser's official page before registering or paying anything. HackUpdate is not affiliated with any of the platforms it links to. © 2026 National Forensic Sciences University.
